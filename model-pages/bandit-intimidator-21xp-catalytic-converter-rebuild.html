What's included

  • Your converter shell, cleaned and inspected

How the rebuild works

  1. Order online. Pick the gas or diesel option on the Bandit rebuild page.
  2. Send your core. We email shipping instructions. Drop-off is by appointment at our Foxboro, MA shop.
  3. Inspection and rebuild. The shell is inspected and the damaged internals are replaced.
  4. Return and install. Your rebuilt converter ships back with new gaskets.

Before you send your core

  • Confirm the engine in your Intimidator 21XP from the machine's serial plate.
  • Take one photo of the whole converter and one straight into the flange.
  • Photograph any tag or stamped numbers on the converter if it has them.
