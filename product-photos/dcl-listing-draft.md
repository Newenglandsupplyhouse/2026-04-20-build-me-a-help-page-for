## Description

**Industrial catalytic converter rebuild and re-pipe service** for DCL Mine-X and similar canister-style converters used on industrial engines in generators, forklifts, mining equipment, and wood chippers.

Send us your existing converter. New England Supply House inspects the shell and substrate, rebuilds damaged internal components where needed, and fabricates and welds new inlet and outlet pipes to your required size and length so the unit bolts back into the existing exhaust run.

### What this service covers
- DCL Mine-X 560GX series and comparable canister-style industrial converters
- Inspection of shell, end caps, and ceramic substrate
- New welded inlet and outlet pipes, cut to your specification
- Flow direction preserved and marked per the original data plate

### Before you order
Photograph the data plate on the side of the converter. It carries the DCL model number (for example 560GX-MT-020D-0C74-01), the serial number, and the flow arrow. Note the inlet and outlet pipe diameters you need. Call New England Supply House at 774-701-6374 with those details and we will confirm fit, price, and turnaround before you ship.
