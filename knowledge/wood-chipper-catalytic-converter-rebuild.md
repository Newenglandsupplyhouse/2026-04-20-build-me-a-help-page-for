# Wood chipper catalytic converter rebuild service

New England Supply House rebuilds catalytic converters for Bandit, Morbark, and Vermeer wood chippers. The customer sends in their existing converter (the "core"). We inspect it, replace the damaged internal ceramic substrates, and return the unit ready to bolt back on. New England Supply House is the only company in the U.S. offering this rebuild for wood chipper converters.

## What is included in a rebuild

- The customer's converter shell, cleaned and inspected
- New ceramic catalyst substrates installed
- Two new exhaust flange gaskets (Fel-Pro 61457), one for the inlet flange and one for the outlet flange

## How to identify which rebuild a customer needs

Ask for, in this order, and stop as soon as the catalog answer is clear:

1. Chipper make, model, and engine (gas or diesel, and the engine model if known). This is on the machine's serial plate. Bandit, Morbark, and Vermeer each use more than one converter version and the engine decides which.
2. A photo of the whole converter and a photo straight into the flange. Flange bolt count (usually four) helps confirm the version.
3. Any data plate or stamped numbers on the converter itself. Many chipper converters have no plate. Some industrial converters (for example DCL Mine-X units) carry a plate with a model number such as 560GX-MT-020D-0C74-01, a serial number, and a flow arrow. If a plate exists, ask for a photo of it.

If the customer cannot say which chipper the converter came off, ask them to call 774-701-6374 with photos. Do not guess the brand from a photo alone.

## Common customer questions

**Do I have to send my old converter?** Yes. This is a rebuild of the customer's own unit, not an off-the-shelf part. Shipping or drop-off instructions are sent after purchase. Drop-off is by appointment at the Foxboro, MA shop.

**Is it as good as new?** The rebuild installs new ceramic substrates and returns the unit to working order. Marketing on the site calls it an OEM-quality rebuild.

**Are gaskets included?** Yes, two new Fel-Pro 61457 flange gaskets ship with every rebuild.

**My chipper is not listed.** The model tables on each product page are the common machines, not a complete list. Tell the customer to call 774-701-6374 and we will confirm whether we can rebuild theirs.

**Do you rebuild other industrial converters (generators, forklifts, DCL Mine-X)?** We have re-piped and rebuilt DCL Mine-X canister converters. Treat this as a call-for-quote service: take the model number from the data plate and the inlet and outlet pipe sizes, and direct the customer to call 774-701-6374. Do not quote a price.
